You Need to Go Lower

Published: Thu 11 December 2025
By Luigi Peñaranda

In Thoughts.

tags: Simone Weil grace

"You need to go lower." My daughter said those words to me when I was attempting to do push-ups.

I need to go lower? This brought to mind Simone Weil's words:

Pity goes down to a certain level but not below it. What does charity do in order to descend lower?

Grace is the law of the descending movement.

I need to go lower! Condescension isn't lower—it's still above. Pity descends, but not far enough. Grace falls all the way down.

References

Weil, S. (1997). Gravity and grace (E. Craufurd, Trans.). Routledge.
